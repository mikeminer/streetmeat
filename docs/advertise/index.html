YOUR MEMETOKEN HERE

Street Meat shows one sponsor card at the top of the Free Play screens. When a slot is booked, your token's name, ticker and logo appear there for every player on the free levels. When no slot is booked, players see the YOUR MEMETOKEN HERE card, which links to this page.

How the in-game ad slot works

  • Where it appears: the top card on the title screen and on the free levels, including the pause and result screens. It is never shown in Season Pass levels or during wallet steps.
  • What players see: your token name, ticker (for example $MEAT) and logo, with the label Sponsored and the note “Sponsored placement. Not investment advice.”
  • Rotation: if several tokens are booked at the same time, the game picks one at random each time it is opened. Up to 12 tokens can rotate at once.
  • Duration: a slot runs for the period you book. It leaves the card automatically when the booking ends.
  • Privacy: the game does not track players for advertisers. It does not send wallet addresses, scores or click data to sponsors.

Book a slot

In-game slots are booked through the featured-token listings on DevFridge Scan. A featured booking there also puts your token on the Street Meat sponsor card for the same period. Slot lengths, current prices and requirements are shown on the booking page before you confirm, and payment is made from your own wallet.

Placement rules

  • The card shows the token name, ticker and logo only. Text is cleaned of markup and shortened if it is too long. Links in token metadata are not shown.
  • Logos must be standard images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or AVIF). Other image formats and very large files are replaced by the first letter of the ticker.
  • A placement is a labelled advertisement. It is not an endorsement, a recommendation or investment advice, and it has no effect on gameplay or the Season Pass.
  • We may remove a placement that is unlawful, misleading, offensive or harmful to players.
